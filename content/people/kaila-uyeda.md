Kaila joined the Ocean & Climate Dynamics lab as an ESS PhD student in fall 2024. She graduated with a B.S. in Math from Haverford College and then worked as a research assistant in the Oceanography Department at Texas A&M. She is interested in studying how the formation and transformation of Antarctic Bottom Water changes over time. To do so, she runs E3SM simulations and analyzes them with tracer budget and mixing diagnostics. Outside of work, she can be found running, climbing, coaching softball, and rooting for the Philadelphia Eagles & San Diego Padres.
