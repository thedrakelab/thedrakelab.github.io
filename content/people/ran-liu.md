Ran's research combines theoretical analysis, ocean observations, and numerical modeling to understand ocean dynamics and climate variability. His work focuses on mesoscale eddies and their roles in ocean circulation and energy transfer in the Southern Ocean. A major focus of his current research interest is developing a new theoretical framework for anomalous water-mass transformation and applying it to understand the underlying mechanisms of El Niño and marine heatwaves.
