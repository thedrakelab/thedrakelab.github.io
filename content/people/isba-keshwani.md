Isba joined the group as a PhD student in the fall of 2023. She received her B.S. in Astrophysics from UCLA in 2023. Her research seeks to understand the processes controlling the ocean's global overturning circulation, specifically the role of differential surface buoyancy forcing in driving deep convection and turbulent mixing. To answer this question she runs high resolution simulations of horizontal convection as an idealized model for Antarctic Bottom Water formation and quantifies turbulent mixing. In her free time she enjoys going to the beach, hiking, cooking, and hanging out with her cat!
